— The Batwa Story

A story rooted in the forest.

For generations, the Batwa lived in close relationship with the forests of the Virunga region, relying on the forest for their livelihood and maintaining a deep cultural and spiritual connection with their ancestral land.

Over time, changes to the forest and its protection led to the displacement of Batwa communities from their ancestral homeland, profoundly affecting their way of life, traditions and connection to the forest.
